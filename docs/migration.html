Migration services

Your archive, moved for you.
The tools stay free.

anvil-migrate and anvil-bulk are open source, and anyone can run them. If your team would rather not, AnvilCAD's maintainer can run the migration for you. It starts with a fixed-price pilot on your own parts, and anything beyond that is quoted only once the pilot shows what your files need.

How it works

Pilot first.
Then a quote.

No price, scope or deadline is promised for a whole archive until a pilot has run on your files. Every archive is different, and the pilot is how both sides find out what yours needs.

  1. 01

    Talk it through

    A short email exchange about what you have: how many parts, assemblies and drawings, which SOLIDWORKS versions, and whether your licences are still active. This costs nothing.

  2. 02

    Paid pilot

    A sample of your parts is migrated for a fixed price agreed before any work starts. You get the AnvilCAD files and the migration-report.csv that says, file by file, what came across and what did not.

  3. 03

    Written quote

    The scope, price and timeline for the rest of the archive are written from what the pilot showed. Anything AnvilCAD cannot do yet is left out of the quote and listed as such.

  4. 04

    Migration

    The work is done as quoted. Your original files are opened read-only and never written to.

What migrates today

Parts, honestly reported.

How the tools work →
  • Licence active

    Parts with their feature trees.

    With a working SOLIDWORKS seat on Windows, parts are rebuilt as editable AnvilCAD features and checked against SOLIDWORKS' own volume. Features that cannot be rebuilt yet come across as geometry, and the report says which.

  • No licence

    Geometry and properties.

    Without SOLIDWORKS, parts come across from STEP exports as solid geometry, with no feature tree. Custom properties are read straight from files saved by SOLIDWORKS 2014 or earlier. Later files need a Document Manager licence key.

  • Not yet

    Assemblies and drawings.

    They are listed in the report but not migrated. They are next on the roadmap, and no quote will include them until AnvilCAD can do them.

What to expect

Nothing promised
that cannot be delivered.

  • No commitments before the pilot. Prices, timelines and coverage for a full archive are set in the written quote, not in the first email.
  • Limits are stated, not hidden. If part of your archive needs something AnvilCAD cannot do yet, the quote says so rather than taking it on.
  • Fixes go back to everyone. Anything improved in AnvilCAD while migrating your files is released in the open repository.
  • No lock-in. The same tools are free to run yourself at any point, before, during or after paid work. See the commercial pledge.
